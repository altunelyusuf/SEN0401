🔗 Interactive Break the Chain Challenge 🔗

A visual game to understand blockchain's immutability.

🎮 How to Play

  1. Click the data of any block (e.g., "Alice pays Bob 1 coin") to edit the transaction.
  2. Change the text and press Enter.
  3. Click the "Start Validation" button.
  4. Follow the steps to see how your change broke the chain!
