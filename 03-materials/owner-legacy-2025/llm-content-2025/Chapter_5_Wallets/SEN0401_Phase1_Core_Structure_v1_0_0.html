Course Overview

What You'll Learn

A comprehensive journey through blockchain wallet technology, from basic concepts to advanced implementations based on Antonopoulos (2017) and modern innovations.

Wallet Fundamentals

Understand what wallets really are: containers for cryptographic keys, not coins. Learn about key pairs, addresses, and the fundamental architecture.

Explore

HD Wallet Architecture

Master Hierarchical Deterministic wallets - derive unlimited keys from a single seed. Understand key derivation, chain codes, and tree structures.

Explore

BIP Standards

Deep dive into Bitcoin Improvement Proposals: BIP-32, BIP-39, BIP-44, BIP-49, BIP-84, and BIP-86. Learn derivation paths and mnemonic codes.

Explore

MPC Wallets

Explore Multi-Party Computation: threshold signatures, FROST protocol, and how institutions secure billions in digital assets without single points of failure.

Learning Objectives

Course Goals

By the end of this course, you will have mastered all aspects of blockchain wallet technology.

After completing this course, you will be able to:

  • Explain why wallets contain keys, not coins, and describe the relationship between private keys, public keys, and addresses.
  • Compare nondeterministic (JBOK) wallets with deterministic and HD wallets, articulating the advantages of each.
  • Implement BIP-32 key derivation and understand the mathematical foundations of HD wallets.
  • Generate and validate BIP-39 mnemonic phrases with proper entropy and checksum verification.
  • Navigate BIP-44/49/84/86 derivation paths and understand their purpose for different address types.
  • Analyze MPC protocols, threshold signatures, and ERC-4337 account abstraction architectures.
