URI - Glossary | MDN Skip to main content Skip to search URI A URI (Uniform Resource Identifier) is a string that refers to a resource. The most common are URLs , which identify the resource by giving its location on the Web. URNs , by contrast, refer to a resource by a name, in a given namespace, such as the ISBN of a book.
