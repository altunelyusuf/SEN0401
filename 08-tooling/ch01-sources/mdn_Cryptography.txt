Cryptography designs and studies algorithms used to encode and decode messages in an insecure environment, and their applications. More than just data confidentiality , cryptography also tackles identification , authentication , non-repudiation , and data integrity . Therefore it also studies usage of cryptographic methods in context, cryptosystems .
