Read ONLY this brief and MOCK_SCHEMA_AND_PLAN.md (same folder). Private output folder: /home/claude/CME/exams/SEN0401/08-tooling/exam/mock/ . Part A. The HTML shell (one self-contained file, no external request, no personal data, no cookies; only localStorage for the student's own progress if the browser allows it, wrapped in try/catch):
1. Embedded bank (the six chapter files merged) and a seeded generator (mulberry32). Mock number k (1..8) uses the k-th slice of a seed-fixed permutation per unit type, so mocks 1..8 share NO item; mock 9 and later wrap around and the page says "items of earlier mocks come back; none is from the real exam". Seed shown on the page and settable; the same seed gives the same mock.
2. A mock follows the shape in the plan (Q1 7 choice, Q2 8 numeric, Q3 matching + matching + ordering, Q4 three fill groups; 100 points; chapter coverage; at most 2 choice items of one chapter in Q1). The shell shuffles the options (letters printed after the shuffle; solutions refer to options by index and the shell prints the letter), distributes right answers over A to E, assigns points: matching 3 and 2 per row, ordering 1 per step, fill 4+4, 4+4, 5+4, one numeric template with can_be_4 raised to 4 points.
3. Student view: the paper layout (Q1 to Q4) with the answer fields on the page: letter buttons A-E, digit boxes for numbers (one digit per box, from the right, at most five), selects for matching, place numbers for ordering, selects for the fill-in blanks. A "Check my answers" button: right = points, wrong or empty = 0 (no deduction, no bonus, two answers in one place impossible); shows the score per question and total, marks every place, and shows after submit for every item: the intention (the "You are asked to" sentence), the ordered steps, why right and why each wrong option or row fails, with the printed letters. Before submit no solution is in the DOM text shown (it is embedded in data but not displayed).
4. Buttons: "Derive a new mock" (next k, new seed), "Print" (print stylesheet: the paper only, answer fields as boxes and lines, solutions off), "Show solutions" only after the check. Phone friendly: one column under 700 px, text at least 16 px, big tap targets, reader-adjustable text size, no horizontal scroll, light/dark neutral. English UI.
5. Scoring function exported separately (scoring.js section) so it can be tested by running code.

Part B. Tests (run the real thing):
6. A Python reference scorer (right = points else 0) and a headless-browser test (use flock /tmp/page_test.lock; nice 10): open a built mock, fill a seeded sheet (some right, some wrong, some empty), press Check, read the score from the page and compare with the Python scorer on the same answers for at least 200 seeded sheets (the scoring function may be driven by the browser or by node on the same function; say which). Blind total of random answers: simulate and compare with the exact value. Planted defects that must be caught: a scorer that gives a deduction, a wrong letter map after the shuffle, two mocks of the same k sharing an item.
7. Disjointness test: derive mocks 1..8 from the full bank (when present) and prove by code that no item id appears in two mocks; mock 9 reuses only ids of mocks 1..8; state the bank size (units and instances) and the number of disjoint mocks (8 expected; report the computed number).
8. View the page: screenshots at 390 px and 1280 px wide of the student view, after the check, and the print view; look at every one and say what you saw.

Fixtures: a verbatim copy of a real item, the same with only numbers changed, the same with two words changed - all must FAIL; a synthetic new item must PASS. Run the gate on the sample bank and, when the six chapter files exist, on the full bank and on the built HTML files (read the embedded bank).
